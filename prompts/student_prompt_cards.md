# Prompt cards (Track B — any chat AI, no coding tools needed)

Copy one card at a time into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar. Paste the piece of data the card asks for.
The goal is not to get "the answer" from the AI; it is to make the AI explain, and then to check it against the tool.

**Card 1 — What am I looking at?**
> Here is the technology matrix A of a small LCA system (paste the "Explain" panel). Explain what each column and row means,
> what the sign convention is, and what solving A·s = f gives us. Then tell me which process will run most often and why.

**Card 2 — Why is activated carbon zero here?**
> In the original paper, replacing the granular activated carbon (GAC) was 36 % of SVE's total impact. In my result (paste the
> by-stage table for climate change) the GAC stage is exactly 0. Explain how activated carbon is made and roughly what its
> cradle-to-gate carbon footprint should be per kg, then tell me what must be missing from the database record for the stage to
> come out as zero, and how I could check.

**Card 3 — Is it fair to use Chinese electricity for a Korean site?**
> The tool uses a Chinese grid mix because the database has no Korean grid. Explain what "geographical representativeness" means in
> ISO 14044, estimate how different Korea's grid carbon intensity is from China's (give sources), and tell me how I could test
> whether this changes the SVE-vs-Biopile conclusion.

**Card 4 — Cut-offs**
> Here is the cut-off list from my LCA (paste it). For each item, say whether it is likely to matter for climate change and for
> toxicity, and which one I should fix first.

**Card 5 — Different method, different answer?**
> The original paper used EDIP 2003 and found SVE worse than Biopile in all nine categories. My tool uses EF 3.1 and finds
> (paste your ratio table). Explain why two methods can rank the same inventories differently, and whether the ranking I got is
> consistent with the paper.

**Card 6 — Check the AI**
> Earlier you said (paste a claim the AI made). The tool shows (paste the number). Were you wrong, or is the tool wrong? Explain how
> you would decide.

**Card 7 — Write it up**
> Write a 150-word results paragraph for a report comparing SVE and Biopile, using these numbers (paste the results table).
> Include one sentence on the main limitation. Do not invent numbers that are not in the table.
