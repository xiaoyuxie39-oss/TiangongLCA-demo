## activated_carbon


**Flow:** `5cad53b5-252a-4e2f-8197-8be465707405` Activated carbon [kg]
**Provider:** `c090f0c3-1e65-4516-bc82-e34b4d25c02c` Carbonization, activation ; Coal activated carbon ; Raw material coal ; Physical activation process ; All sizes; NESPS2
**Conversion:** kg × 1 — Paper prints GAC mass in kg (SVE 18,000 kg; biopile 1,368 kg); the TianGong flow's reference property is Mass in kg, so no conversion is needed.  
*Source:* Identity: Jeong & Suh 2011 Tables 2-3 report kg; snapshot flow 5cad53b5 prop=Mass unit=kg (reference/snapshot.py flow).
**Alternatives:** `a83ed2c4`, `8e839591`, `4b8ba0f1`

**Confidence:** low  ·  **Verifier:** accepted

### Mapper's reasoning

Technology match: the paper's GAC LCI (Bayer et al. 2005) is hard-coal GAC made by steam (physical) activation, and vapour-phase GAC for VOC off-gas is typically coal- or coconut-based physically activated carbon; c090f0c3 is the CN-2019 coal / physical-activation dataset, geo CN and recent, with real elementary flows (per t AC: SO2 2.78 kg, NOx 3.52 kg, PM 0.94 kg). TianGong splits the coal route into four unit operations that all have ref output 1000 kg AC (crushing/sieving b74addfe, carbonization/activation c090f0c3, transport/storage d69afaef, flushing 6ecb514c); the matrix can link only one, but the other three carry almost nothing (0.0023 kg PM, no elementary flows, 0.0064 kg COD per t), so choosing c090f0c3 alone reproduces >99% of what TianGong has for coal AC and does not consume its own reference flow. What it misrepresents: like every NESPS2 dataset it has NO inputs at all (no coal feedstock, steam, electricity or gas), so the energy-intensive manufacture that makes GAC 36% of SVE's normalised impact in the paper is absent and the tool will show GWP ~0 for GAC against roughly 100-200 t CO2 implied by the paper (literature cradle-to-gate GWP of coal-based virgin GAC is of order 8-11 kg CO2-eq/kg, e.g. Bayer et al. 2005 and ecoinvent 'activated carbon, granular from hard coal' - cited from memory, verify before quoting). Suggested handling for the teaching exercise: keep c090f0c3 as the linked provider for direct process emissions and present the missing manufacture energy as a documented, optional add-on (~10 kg CO2-eq/kg GAC, i.e. ~180 t for SVE and ~14 t for biopile) in the discussion of why the TianGong result diverges from Table 5. Alternatives: a83ed2c4 (fruit-shell, physical activation, single gate-to-gate process, CN 2019) is the nearest biomass analogue (coconut-shell GAC is also used for vapour adsorption) and the natural sensitivity case; 8e839591 (NO 2011, Sparrevik et al., black box) is the only dataset with any input linked (electricity 1.36 MJ/kg) but its coal 1.89 kg/kg and road transport 0.905 kg/kg are cut-offs and it has no elementary flows, so it is not better; 4b8ba0f1 (wood chips, chemical activation) is a different product (chemical activation is mainly for powdered AC) and emits 254 kg PM/t, which would dominate the PM category for no good reason.

### Verifier's verdict

ACCEPTED. Every check passes on independent CLI verification: the flow is the kg Product flow TianGong's own curators used for this paper, the identity conversion is correct, c090f0c3 is a genuine producer (ref output 1000 kg AC) with direct SO2/NOx/PM emissions, no inputs, no self-consumption, and all three alternatives are valid producers of the same flow. Searches in English and Chinese found no candidate the mapper missed. The one serious weakness, zero upstream energy so GWP of GAC is ~0 against the paper's 36% share, is intrinsic to every TianGong dataset for this flow, is honestly disclosed in the mapper's entry, and must be made explicit in the lesson; the duplicate-row double counting in c090f0c3 is a second caveat to mention.

### Issues raised by the verifier

- Known and correctly disclosed by the mapper: c090f0c3 (like all seven producers except 8e839591) has no product or energy inputs, so cradle-to-gate manufacture energy/CO2 is absent and GWP for GAC will be ~0 in the tool, whereas the case doc (docs/04, line 69-84) expects 'operation + GAC dominate SVE'. This is a dataset limitation, not a mapping error; the suggested add-on (~8-11 kg CO2-eq/kg GAC, verify against Bayer et al. 2005 / ecoinvent before quoting) must be surfaced in the lesson or students will read a silent zero.
- c090f0c3 carries two rows per elementary flow (PM 0.443+0.497, SO2 1.3695+1.407, NOx 1.556+1.96 kg/t, Exhaust gas 56687+57195 m3/t); the matrix sums them, so direct emissions are plausibly ~2x a single census coefficient row. Verified in the snapshot; the mapper flagged it.
- The coal route is split into four NESPS2 fragments with identical ref output (b74addfe, c090f0c3, d69afaef, 6ecb514c); only one can be linked. Verified that the three unlinked fragments carry essentially nothing (PM ~0.002 kg/t, an untyped 'Waste water' row, and Exhaust gas product outputs that the matrix ignores), so the loss is negligible.
- 8e839591 (NO 2011) is the only producer with any linked upstream (electricity 1.359 MJ/kg -> 34 producers), but its coal 1.890 kg/kg and 'Road Transport' 0.905 kg (mass unit for a transport service) are cut-offs and it has no elementary flows; at CN grid intensity it would give only ~0.2-0.3 kg CO2-eq/kg GAC, i.e. a few percent of literature cradle-to-gate GWP, so it is not clearly better than c090f0c3 and the mapper's ranking stands.
- Non-reference product outputs 'Exhaust gas' (14d56ab9, m3) and the untyped 'Waste water' flow 72721c4e will appear in the tool's unlinked-outputs list for every NESPS2 provider; harmless but noisy.
- Both KR-2011 curated processes (2f2acd1d, 517a7de8) consume this flow at exactly 18,000 kg and 1,368 kg, independently confirming flow choice and amounts; 2f2acd1d also emits 18,000 kg spent-GAC solid waste that the foreground here does not model (end-of-life of GAC is out of scope of the mapping).

### Data-quality notes

(1) All six NESPS2 producers are direct-emission coefficient datasets (第二次全国污染源普查产排污核算系数手册): zero product or energy inputs, only air/water pollutant outputs, so cradle-to-gate energy, CO2 and feedstock are entirely missing. (2) Each NESPS2 process carries repeated exchange rows for the same elementary flow: c090f0c3 has two rows each for PM, SO2, NOx and Exhaust gas; a83ed2c4 has three identical SO2 rows (0.47 kg) and five PM rows; 4b8ba0f1 has five PM rows summing to 253.7 kg/t. These look like several census coefficient rows (sub-technologies or control levels) loaded side by side; the matrix sums them, so c090f0c3's emissions are probably ~2x a single-technology value. (3) Every NESPS2 producer also emits a non-reference product output 'Exhaust gas' (14d56ab9, m3, ~114,000 m3/t for c090f0c3) and the biomass/flushing ones a 'Waste water' flow 72721c4e that has no type or unit in the snapshot; per the matrix conventions these are ignored for A and show up as unlinked outputs, harmless but noisy. (4) The coal route is four fragments with identical ref output (1000 kg AC); linking one loses the others, but the loss is negligible as quantified above. (5) 8e839591 (NO 2011): ref output 47,600,000 kg; per kg AC inputs coal 1.890 kg (flow 0e894e31, 0 producers), electricity 1.359 MJ (34 producers), 'Road Transport' 0.905 kg (flow 9462b645, 0 producers, mass unit for a transport service is a unit mismatch); no elementary flows of its own. (6) Flows 'Coal-based powdered activated carbon' (0212cd93) and 'Biochar based powdered activated carbon' (8273217c) exist but have 0 producers; 'Granular active carbon unit' (87f1d444) is an infrastructure item in Item(s), wrong scope. (7) The two KR-2011 curated processes (2f2acd1d SVE 18,000 kg; 517a7de8 biopile 1,368 kg) confirm the hint flow and amounts; 2f2acd1d also emits 18,000 kg 'Waste (solid)' for spent GAC, which the foreground here does not model.

## ammonium_sulphate


**Flow:** `9874382d-672c-4601-a3ce-9a4ae21e663b` Ammonia [kg]
**Provider:** `9c8dd7aa-499c-4c33-9df0-da4876f6139e` Bio-based ammonia production ; Ammonia ; Electrolysis-based route ; N2, H2
**Conversion:** kg × 0.258 — Stoichiometric ammonia content of ammonium sulphate. (NH4)2SO4 is made by neutralising ammonia with sulfuric acid: 2 NH3 + H2SO4 -> (NH4)2SO4. Molar masses: (NH4)2SO4 = 132.13 g/mol, NH3 = 17.03 g/mol, so 2 x 17.03 / 132.13 = 0.258 kg NH3 per kg ammonium sulphate (N content 21.2 %). The co-reactant 0.742 kg H2SO4/kg and the neutralisation/drying step are deliberately left out (no usable sulfuric-acid provider; both are minor next to ammonia synthesis). 2,708 kg AS -> 698.7 kg NH3.  
*Source:* Stoichiometry from IUPAC standard atomic weights; process route per Ullmann's Encyclopedia of Industrial Chemistry, 'Ammonium Compounds' (ammonium sulfate from NH3 + H2SO4), which is also the route ecoinvent models as 'ammonium sulfate production'.
**Alternatives:** none

**Confidence:** low  ·  **Verifier:** accepted

### Mapper's reasoning

TianGong has the correct flow 'Ammonium sulfate' (6f4bede8, the curators' hint, consumed at 2,708 kg in their own KR 2011 soil-conditioning process 38ea5d3c) but no process produces it: the flow's only listed producer, 65dfa04a 'REO mining / in situ leaching', is a chain fragment whose reference exchange is an INPUT of 7 kg ammonium sulfate plus 30.96 MJ electricity and no REO output, i.e. it consumes its own reference flow and must be rejected. Six other processes (glyphosate 10670104, coke 4ef07c9c, secondary-aluminium slag 5a208654/ec794d78, REO LCI 68420e06, carbon anode b7e981fd) emit ammonium sulfate only as a non-reference by-product, which the matrix convention ignores as 'unlinked outputs'. Every other nitrogen-chemical route in the snapshot (NESPS2 pollution-census datasets for synthetic ammonia x17, urea x11, ammonium nitrate x2, compound fertiliser x4, sulfuric acid x4) is a pollutant-only stub with zero product or energy inputs, so using one would record a few grams of PM and N-to-water and nothing of the ammonia synthesis that dominates the burden; the NESPS2 'Synthetic ammonia' reference flow 82980072 is not even present in the flow table. The only gate-to-gate ammonia dataset with linked inputs is 9c8dd7aa (Haber-Bosch, CN): per 1,000 kg NH3 it takes 7,200 MJ electricity (CN mix, linked), 177 kg hydrogen (linked, 6 producers) and 822.5 kg N2 (cut-off, no producer), stoichiometrically consistent. Linking the item as 0.258 kg NH3 per kg (NH4)2SO4 captures the dominant part of ammonium-sulphate production (ammonia synthesis) and is the physically justified proxy for a nitrogen nutrient. What it misrepresents: (1) it is an electrolysis-route black box, not the coal-based ammonia typical of China or the NG-based ammonia typical of Korea; (2) its hydrogen input resolves by the geo/year/uuid rule to SMR 615f558a (CN 2022), whose natural-gas inputs (0.48 MJ + 0.12 kg NG per kg H2) look under-scaled by one to two orders of magnitude, so upstream burden is understated; the alternative hydrogen producer 9d85fcde (water electrolysis, 18 MJ/kg H2) is similarly ~10x too low; (3) the 0.742 kg H2SO4 and neutralisation/crystallisation energy are omitted. Net effect: expect roughly 0.3 kg CO2e per kg ammonium sulphate from this chain, versus about 0.5-0.7 kg CO2e/kg for the NH3+H2SO4 route in process databases and 1.5-2.5 for coal-based Chinese N fertiliser, i.e. an underestimate of perhaps 2-5x, but the right order of magnitude and far better than a zero cut-off for a 2.7 t input. For a sensitivity exercise, the hybrid alternative is the CEEIO 2018 sector process a0726d31 'Chemical fertilizers' (ref flow d466dbb6 in EUR, 'US 2000 bulk prices'), which has real elementary flows but needs a price conversion (~0.1 EUR/kg AS) and pulls the whole 153-sector IO block into A; it is a different flow so it is not listed as an alternative uuid. If a documented external factor is preferred, a literature value for ammonium sulphate production (e.g. ecoinvent 'ammonium sulfate production, RoW') can be added as a foreground emission instead.

TianGong has no welded-mesh, steel-wire or wire-drawing production dataset: the hint flow has no producer, the four 'Steel Wire' producers are cable-assembly fragments (the flow is really 'Steel Wire,Cable'), and the NESPS2 wire-rod (eef7933a), cold-drawn bar (daabaa23) and hot-rolled bar datasets are pollution-census emission coefficients with no material or energy inputs and no CO2, so used alone they would drop the entire steelmaking burden. Welded mesh is >98% low-carbon steel by mass, so the item is modelled as generic steel via the kg/m2 conversion. Among the 5 producers of the generic 'Steel' flow (the flow 62 background processes consume, including two Korean datasets), 35a9a83f is the only self-contained cradle-to-gate inventory with physically plausible magnitudes: 1.72 kg CO2/kg, 26.7 MJ/kg, 4.0 g SO2/kg, 1.6 g PM2.5/kg (Yang et al. 2018, Donghai Bridge offshore wind farm). What it misrepresents: (1) Chinese 2009 structural steel for a wind farm instead of Korean welded wire fabric; (2) 3.7 g/kg is booked on the elementary flow 'nitrous oxide' (29061478, GWP 273) - almost certainly NOx in the source paper - so climate change comes out at about 2.73 kg CO2e/kg instead of about 1.7 (the paper's ecoinvent 2.1 reinforcing-steel background is about 1.5), while acidification and particulate impacts are understated because that flow and the PM2.5 flow 4d9a8790 (CF 0) carry no usable factor; (3) its energy input sits on the placeholder 'Energy, geothermal, converted', which no LCIA method characterises; (4) wire drawing and mesh welding are omitted - the NESPS2 coefficients show they add only ~0.06 g SO2 and ~0.13 g NOx per kg, negligible next to steelmaking.
