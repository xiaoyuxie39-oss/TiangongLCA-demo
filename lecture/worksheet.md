# Worksheet — SVE vs Biopile with TianGong data

Functional unit: remediation of 1,000 m³ TPH-contaminated soil (Jeong & Suh 2011). Tool: the web page (QR code / `web/index.html`).
Record your answers; each group submits them on the classroom site (see "Hand in" at the end).

## Everyone (Track C is exactly this list)

1. With default providers, write down climate change (kg CO₂-eq) for SVE and for Biopile, and the ratio.
2. For SVE, which two stages dominate climate change? What share do they have together? The paper says operation + GAC = 96 %.
   Is GAC one of your two stages? If not, find the activated-carbon provider in the provider log, then switch the impact category
   to Acidification and look at the GAC stage again. What does that dataset contain, and what is missing from it?
3. Which single elementary flow contributes most to SVE's freshwater ecotoxicity? Which process emits it?
4. Change the electricity provider to a province of your choice, **in both SVE and Biopile**: the dropdown belongs to one system,
   and a comparison needs the same grid on both sides. Note the province and the new SVE and Biopile totals.
   Does the SVE/Biopile ratio change much?
5. Open the cut-off list and the provider log. Which cut-off would you fix first, and why? Which provider choice would you overrule, and why?
6. Look at the "Explain" panel. How many columns does the full technology matrix have? How many of them are foreground stages (system + stages)?
7. Table 4 of the paper reports 5.75 t CO₂-eq for SVE; Table 5 reports 360 t CO₂. Which order of magnitude does the tool support?
   Name one thing the tool leaves out that would move its number towards one of the two tables.
